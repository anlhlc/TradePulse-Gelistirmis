📊 Market Data
24h High/Low: Shows the highest and lowest price levels in the last 24 hours. These levels can act as daily support and resistance.

Volume: Represents the total amount of coins traded in the last 24 hours. High volume indicates an active market and a potentially strong trend.

OI (Open Interest): The total number of open long and short positions in the market. Rising OI = new money entering; falling OI = positions closing.

Long/Short Ratio: Shows the ratio of long positions to total positions. Above 50% suggests bullish bias; below 50% suggests bearish bias.

Funding Rate: The periodic fee paid by longs to shorts. Positive = longs pay; Negative = shorts pay.

Order Book: Distribution of buy and sell orders. Green areas indicate support; red areas indicate resistance.
📈 Price Chart & Indicators
Price Chart: Candles show the open, high, low, and close prices. Green = bullish, Red = bearish.

RSI (14): A momentum oscillator ranging from 0 to 100. 70+ = overbought (sell signal), 30- = oversold (buy signal).

StochRSI: A faster version of RSI. 80+ = sell zone, 20- = buy zone. Generates many signals.

Bollinger Bands: Shows the normal price range. Upper/lower bands act as support/resistance. Narrowing = lower volatility = move likely soon.

ATR (14): Measures volatility. High ATR = wider stop-loss needed. Low ATR = tighter stop-loss is enough.

Session Summary: Shows the daily average price (VWAP), session high/low, and volatility.
🔧 Trading Tools
PnL Calculator: Calculates profit/loss. As leverage increases, gains or losses multiply. ROE = return on equity.

Liquidation Calculator: Calculates the price at which the position will be automatically closed. As leverage increases, liquidation risk rises.

Position Size: Calculates the optimal position size for risk management. Determines the maximum position based on the stop-loss.

Correlation Matrix: Shows the relationship between coins. Green = same direction, Red = opposite direction.

Alerts: Lets you receive notifications when price or RSI levels are reached.
⚖️ Long/Short Ratio Explanation
Long Position: Buy positions opened with the expectation that price will rise. Profit when price rises, loss when it falls.

Short Position: Sell positions opened with the expectation that price will fall. Profit when price falls, loss when it rises.

Long/Short Ratio: The ratio of long positions to total open positions. Above 50% = bullish dominance, below 50% = bearish dominance.

Yorumlama:
• 60%+ Long = Market is overly bullish, be careful
• 40%- Short = Market is overly bearish, may be a buy opportunity
• Around 50% = Balance, indecisive market
📊 Open Interest (OI)
Open Interest (OI): Total number of open positions in the market. Sum of both long and short positions.

If OI is Rising: New money is entering the market. This suggests the trend may continue.

If OI is Falling: Positions are being closed. This suggests the trend may weaken.

OI + Price Divergence: OI is rising but price is flat/falling = potential correction signal. Distribution risk.

OI + Price Alignment: OI is rising and price is increasing = strong bullish trend. Accumulation.
💰 Funding Rate Explanation
Funding Rate: A periodic fee paid by long positions to short positions. Paid every 8 hours.

Positive Funding: Longs are paying shorts. The market is generally bullish. Shorts receive funding from longs.

Negative Funding: Shorts are paying longs. The market is generally bearish. Longs receive funding from shorts.

High Funding: Extremely bullish sentiment. The market may have topped. Be careful.

Low/Negative Funding: Extremely bearish sentiment. The market may have bottomed. Could be a buying opportunity.
📋 Order Book Explanation
Order Book: Shows the distribution of pending buy and sell orders by price level.

Green Areas (Bids): Buy orders. If price drops below these levels, buyers execute orders = support.

Red Areas (Asks): Sell orders. If price rises above these levels, sellers execute orders = resistance.

Order Wall: If a large number of orders are waiting at one level = strong support or resistance.

Usage: Price may react at levels with large order clusters.
📊 RSI (Relative Strength Index)
What is RSI: Momentum oscillator. Measures the speed and magnitude of price changes. Values range from 0 to 100.

Formula: Calculated as (Gain / (Gain + Loss)) x 100. The default period is 14 candles.

Buy Signal: When RSI drops below 30, it enters the oversold zone = potential buying opportunity.

Sell Signal: When RSI rises above 70, it enters the overbought zone = potential selling opportunity.

Caution: In strong trends, RSI can stay above 70 or below 30 for a long time. Trading based only on RSI is risky.
📊 Bollinger Bands
Bollinger Bands: A volatility indicator showing the statistically normal range of price movement.

Middle Band: 20-period simple moving average (SMA). Works as a trend indicator.

Upper Band: SMA + (2 x Standard Deviation). Potential resistance level.

Lower Band: SMA - (2 x Standard Deviation). Potential support level.

Squeeze: When the bands narrow, volatility drops = a large move may come soon.

Breakout: When price moves outside the bands, the trend may continue, but the opposite can also happen.
📊 ATR (Average True Range)
What is ATR: An indicator that measures market volatility (movement range). It does not show direction.

Formula: The True Range of each candle is calculated as the greatest of: (High-Low), (High-Previous Close), (Previous Close-Low).

Yorumlama:
• High ATR = Market is volatile = wider stop-loss needed
• Low ATR = Market is calm = tighter stop-loss is enough

Practical Use: When setting a stop-loss, you can use ATR x 1.5 or 2 as the distance from price.
📐 Regression Channel
Regression Channel: Draws the best-fit linear trend line for price data and standard deviation channels around it.

Middle Line: The optimal trend line calculated using the Least Squares Method.

Upper/Lower Band: Upper and lower channels at a standard deviation (stdDev) distance.

StdDev Multiplier: Default 2 = 95% of prices remain inside the channel.

Usage: Upper band = resistance, lower band = support. Selling near the upper band and buying near the lower band may be considered.

Candle Count: Determines how much historical data is used. Longer lookback = wider channel.
🔢 Fibonacci Seviyeleri
Fibonacci Retracement: A tool used to identify retracement levels in price movement.

Golden Ratio (0.618): A ratio commonly found in nature and markets. Strong support/resistance level.

Key Levels:
• 0.236 (%23.6) - Weak support/resistance
• 0.382 (%38.2) - Orta seviye
• 0.500 (%50) - Midpoint
• 0.618 (%61.8) - Golden ratio, strong level
• 0.786 (%78.6) - Strong retracement

Usage: In an uptrend, a correction may find support at 61.8%. In a downtrend, a recovery may face resistance at 61.8%.
💰 PnL Calculator Explanation
PnL (Profit and Loss): Calculates potential profit or loss.

Formulas:
• PnL = (Exit - Entry) × Position Size × Leverage
• ROE = (PnL / Marjin) × 100

Long Trade: Profit is made if price rises.

Short Trade: Profit is made if price falls.

Caution: Leverage multiplies both gains and losses. With 10x leverage, a 10% move = 100% profit or loss.
⚠️ Liquidation Calculator
Liquidation: Automatic closure of a position due to losses. Happens because of insufficient margin.

Liquidation Price: The price level at which the position will be closed automatically.

Isolated Margin: Each position uses separate margin. Even if one position is liquidated, the others are not affected.

Cross Margin: The entire account balance is used as margin for a position.

Risk: As leverage increases, the liquidation price gets closer to the entry price.

Safer Trading: Leverage should be kept at 10x or below. A stop-loss should always be used.
📊 Position Size Calculator
Position Size: Calculates the most suitable position size for risk management.

Risk Amount: The maximum loss amount you are willing to take on the trade.

Stop-Loss: The price level at which you accept the loss.

Formula:
Position Size = Risk Amount / |Entry Price - Stop Loss|

Example: $100 risk, $50000 entry, $49500 stop = 100 / 500 = 0.002 BTC position

Rule: No more than 1-2% of total capital should be risked on a single trade.
🔗 Korelasyon Matrisi
Correlation: Measures the relationship between the price movements of two assets. Values range from -1 to +1.

Positive (+1): Two coins move in the same direction. If BTC rises, the altcoin also rises.

Negative (-1): Two coins move in opposite directions. When one rises, the other falls.

Zero (0): No relationship. Coins move independently.

Usage:
• Positive correlation = portfolio diversification is harder
• Negative correlation = good for risk distribution
• Low correlation = independently moving assets

Practical Use:

🚀 Advanced Signals
OI Divergence: Price and OI moving in opposite directions. If price rises while OI falls = potential correction.

Funding Spike: A sudden rise in the funding rate. Extremely bullish sentiment = possible top.

Volatility Squeeze: Bollinger Bands narrowing. Low volatility = a large move may come soon.

Yorumlama:
• Bearish divergence = sell signal
• Bullish divergence = buy signal
• Funding spike + high price = be careful
• Follow the breakout direction after a squeeze
